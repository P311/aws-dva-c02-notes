# Skill 2 — Short-Answer Q&A with Follow-Up

## English

**Purpose**: test genuine understanding of a module's notes, not just recognition — and specifically catch the gap between "I recognize this if I see it" and "I could explain this from scratch."

**Input**: the completed notes for a given module.

**Process**: 
- Generate short-answer questions drawn from the notes — not multiple choice, not yes or no. 
- After each answer, the skill can follow up: asking for more detail on a vague answer, probing an edge case the first answer didn't cover, or asking a "why" question when the answer was correct but the reasoning behind it wasn't demonstrated. 
- When a user admits to not clearly understanding a concept or asks a clarifying question, provide a clear explanation using simple language and examples.

**Output**: a running sense of which concepts are genuinely understood versus only pattern-matched, surfaced through the conversation itself rather than a score.

**Design notes**: this skill is closer to active recall practice than a formal quiz. 

---

## 中文

**用途**：检验对一个模块笔记的真实理解程度，而不只是"看到了觉得眼熟"——尤其是要抓住"看到能认出来"和"能从零讲清楚"之间的落差。

**输入**：某个模块已经写好的笔记（Skill 1 的产出）。

**处理过程**：
- 根据笔记生成简答题——不是选择题，不是判断题。
- 每次回答之后，这个技能可以继续追问：对模糊的回答要求补充细节，探查第一次回答没覆盖到的边界情况，或者在答案本身是对的但没展示出推理过程时追问"为什么"。
- 当用户承认对某个概念理解不清或提出反问时，用比较浅显的语言加上举例进行明确解释


**输出**：不是一个分数，而是在对话过程中自然浮现出来的判断——哪些概念是真正理解了的，哪些只是模式匹配出来的。

**设计说明**：这个技能更接近主动回忆式的练习，而不是正式的测验。
