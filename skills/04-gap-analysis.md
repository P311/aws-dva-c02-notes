# Skill 4 — Performance Analysis & Gap-Filling

## English

**Purpose**: close the loop — instead of re-studying every module equally regardless of how well it was already known, identify specifically where the weak spots were and generate material targeted at exactly those gaps.

**Input**: the record of answers from skill 3 or a Tutorials Dojo mock exam.

**Process**: review which questions were missed or answered shakily, look for a pattern rather than treating each miss in isolation (e.g. consistently mixing up Multi-AZ and Read Replicas, or repeatedly getting DynamoDB capacity-mode questions wrong), and generate supplementary explanation or targeted practice focused on that specific pattern rather than a generic re-read of the whole module.

**Output**: a short list of confirmed weak areas per module, plus supplementary notes or questions aimed directly at them.

**Design notes**: not just "look back at wrong answers," is the pattern-finding step. A single missed question might be a one-off slip, but the same *kind* of mistake showing up across several questions is a real gap worth addressing directly, and that distinction requires looking at answers in aggregate rather than one at a time. This was the mechanism that made review time scale with actual weakness rather than with total syllabus size.

## 中文

**用途**：把整个学习闭环收尾——不是不分青红皂白地把每个模块都重新学一遍，而是具体找出薄弱的地方在哪，然后针对性地生成材料去补这些具体的缺口。

**输入**：某个模块或一组模块里积累下来的答题记录（既包括 Skill 2 的简答题问答过程，也包括 Skill 3 的选择题练习结果）。

**处理过程**：回顾哪些题答错了或者答得不够扎实，找的是模式而不是孤立地看每一道错题（比如反复把 Multi-AZ 和 Read Replica 搞混，或者一再答错 DynamoDB capacity mode 相关的题），然后针对这个具体模式生成补充讲解或专项练习，而不是把整个模块泛泛地重读一遍。

**输出**：每个模块确认下来的一小份薄弱点清单，加上直接针对这些点的补充笔记或题目。

**设计说明**：不是简单地"回头看看错题"，关键在于找模式这一步。单独一道错题可能只是失误，但同一类错误反复出现在好几道题上，那就是一个值得直接处理的真实缺口，而这个区分需要把答题结果放在一起看，而不是一道一道单独看。正是这个机制让复习的时间花在真正的薄弱点上，而不是随着整个考纲的篇幅线性增长。
